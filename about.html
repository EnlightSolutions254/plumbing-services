Our Story

Founded on a simple idea: show up, and do it right

Plumbing Services was founded in Nairobi by a small team of master plumbers who were tired of an industry known for missed appointments and inflated invoices. We set out to build a company that answers the phone at 2am, arrives when it says it will, and quotes a fair, fixed price before touching a single pipe.

More than a decade later, that founding promise hasn't changed — even as we've grown to serve thousands of homes and dozens of commercial clients across the city.
